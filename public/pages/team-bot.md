## Unique Features

### /live - Real-Time In-Play Monitoring
Monitor over bets as the match unfolds. Grades overs early when thresholds are hit - catch wins before full-time.

### /freeze - Sky Acca Freeze Alerts
A+ to C-rated opportunities to bet on Score First AND Lead Anytime before lineups confirm. Lock in prices before the market reacts.

### /streak - Historical Pattern Detection
Scans today's fixtures to find the longest ongoing statistical streaks. Home/Away splits calculated separately.

### Predicted Lines
Comprehensive projections for every match across 15+ metrics.

### /teammost - Team Most Statistics
Which team will have MORE corners, MORE cards, MORE shots? Useful for "Team Most" bookmaker markets.

### XI Alerts
Lineup confirmation notifications 75 minutes before kickoff.

## Commands Reference

- `/today` / `/tomorrow` - Browse fixtures
- `/team [name]` - Deep-dive a specific team
- `/league [name]` - Browse by league
- `/fixture [match]` - Full value analysis (Club Legend)
- `/value` - Top value plays (Club Legend)
- `/stats` - Global top projections by stat category
- `/live` - In-play monitoring
- `/freeze` - Sky Acca Freeze opportunities
- `/streak` - Historical streak detection
- `/top` - Top teams globally by stat
- `/topteam [team]` - Team's ranking across all stats
- `/topleague [league]` - League leaders by stat
- `/teammost` - Teams most likely to lead specific stats
- `/weather` - Weather impact on fixtures
- `/settings` / `/markets` / `/unmute` - Configuration
- `/track` - Bet history
