## What is Arbitrage Betting?

Arbitrage betting places bets on all possible outcomes of an event at different bookmakers, exploiting odds differences to guarantee profit regardless of result.

**Example:**
- Bet365: Over 2.5 Goals @ 2.10
- Pinnacle: Under 2.5 Goals @ 2.05
- Stake £48.78 on Over + £51.22 on Under = £100 total
- **Guaranteed: £102.44 (2.44% profit) regardless of result**

## Three Stake Modes

| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| Standard | Equal profit regardless of outcome |
| RF High | Break even on likely outcome, big profit if underdog wins |
| RF Low | Break even on unlikely outcome, moderate profit on likely winner |

## Executing an Arb (Step-by-Step)

1. Receive alert - note both bookmakers, odds, pre-calculated stakes
2. Open both bookmakers simultaneously (two tabs or devices)
3. Verify current odds match the alert (use Edit Odds button if moved)
4. Place smaller stake first
5. Place second bet immediately - arbs close within 60–120 seconds

## Alert Buttons

- **✏️ Edit Stake** - Modify one side, other auto-recalculates
- **💰 Edit Total Stake** - Change total, bot re-splits automatically
- **📚 Edit Odds** - Update if moved, bot recalculates margin
- **Standard/RF High/RF Low** - Switch stake strategy

## Avoiding Gubbing (Account Restrictions)

- Round stakes to nearest £5/£10 - avoid exact calculator numbers
- Don't always bet maximum allowed
- Mix in occasional non-arb bets
- Don't withdraw immediately after every arb
